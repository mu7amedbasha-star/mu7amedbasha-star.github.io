Strategy Lab Publisher

Internal publishing tool of the Strategy Lab (مختبر الاستراتيجية) YouTube channel

Strategy Lab Publisher is a private command-line application used only by the owner of the Strategy Lab YouTube channel. It sets the metadata, thumbnails, captions and publish times of the channel's own videos, posts the channel owner's own comment replies, and reads the channel's own analytics. It has no other users and no public interface.
